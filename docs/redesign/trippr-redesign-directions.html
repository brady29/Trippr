Trippr · redesign · for review

Swiss Rail, with a folding icon menu

You picked Swiss Rail. The phone's bottom tab bar is gone: navigation now lives in one menu on the left at every screen size, with an icon for each page. Use the fold button at the top of the menu to switch between icons only and icons with labels. On a phone the folded rail stays beside the content and slides out over it. The other directions are still here for comparison.

Night mode is new in every direction.

Ten flat tabs become four groups

Today every tool sits in one row that scrolls sideways even on a laptop, and the route editor lives outside the trip. The new structure groups pages by what the group is doing and keeps the chosen route in view.
